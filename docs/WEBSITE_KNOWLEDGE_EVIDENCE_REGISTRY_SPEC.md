# Prometheus Website Knowledge & Evidence Registry Spec

Status: operational website specification; not a canonical amendment; not site-performance evidence; not legal, financial, tax, investment or scientific advice.

Purpose: align public website, resource routing, product language and intake funnels with the Prometheus canon hierarchy, claims discipline and current evidence boundaries.

## 1. Source-of-truth hierarchy

The website must distinguish, visibly and consistently:

1. Current / ratified canon — only a governance-approved, signed canonical release.
2. Candidate amendments — proposed revisions under governance review.
3. Evidence and pilot materials — operational design, protocol, preregistration or field-evidence documents.
4. Institutional diligence materials — derived materials for funders, partners or counterparties.
5. Vision materials — philosophical or public-facing narrative, non-governing.
6. Archive and provenance — superseded or legacy artifacts preserved for historical and cryptographic continuity.

No resource may silently move upward in this hierarchy.

## 2. Current release boundary

The currently referenced controlling signed release line is v1.1.2-genesis in `Permaculture-DAO/prometheus-canon`.

Later local documents, working drafts, candidate relational-living-system amendments, investor decks, website pages or operational packs are candidates only until they pass the applicable amendment, review, hash, signing and release procedure.

Website copy must not describe any candidate file as current canon, ratified canon, validated performance, certification, market readiness or investment readiness.

## 3. Candidate v7.1 relational amendment boundary

The September 2026 relational-living-system material may be presented as:

**Candidate Canon v7.1 — Relational Living-System Amendment**

Permitted status badge:

**CANDIDATE · GOVERNANCE REVIEW · PRE-PILOT · NON-OFFERING**

Permitted description:

A proposed amendment introducing a place-contextualised relational living-system frame while preserving the non-bypassable evidence-to-institutional sequence.

Forbidden descriptions include Current Canon, Ratified Canon, Final Canon, Validated Canon, certified methodology, production-ready methodology or investment-ready architecture.

## 4. Evidence and pilot boundary

The Minimum Evidence Pilot may be described as a pre-pilot evidence architecture combining place and relational context, system boundary, baseline, comparator, preregistered hypotheses, field/laboratory measurements, provenance, confidence and independent review.

It must not be described as proof of site performance, certification, investment readiness, ecological credit creation or verified regenerative value.

## 5. Product scope boundary

Current commercial tiers may be positioned as access/workspace/preparation products only:

- Explorer — free discovery access.
- Builder Founding Access — builder-oriented workspace and onboarding access.
- Pilot Preparation Access — pilot-preparation workspace, not field pilot execution.
- Consortium Workspace Access — institutional workspace and coordination access, not consortium implementation.
- Reviewer — complimentary or invitation-based review participation, never pay-to-validate.

Scientific field implementation, laboratories, independent review, custom MRV, grant work-package design, legal structuring and institutional consortium execution are separately scoped professional or institutional engagements.

Payment for an access product does not create scientific validation, pilot admission, consortium acceptance, PRU value, RAP maturity, token rights, investor rights, ecological credits, land rights or revenue rights.

## 6. Consortium funnel boundary

The consortium pathway should be qualification-first:

Form submission -> institutional resource review -> qualification -> consortium-fit discussion -> appropriate access or separately scoped proposal.

The website must not imply that a low-cost subscription purchases an institutional consortium programme or field implementation.

## 7. Resource registry structure

Recommended public URL: `/resources/`

Recommended title: **Prometheus Knowledge & Evidence Registry**

Every resource card should show status, title, version, date, audience, purpose, claims boundary and link/access route.

Recommended sections:

1. Current / Ratified Canon
2. Candidate Amendments
3. Evidence & Pilot
4. Governance & Decision Records
5. Institutional Diligence
6. Vision
7. Archive & Provenance

## 8. Archive and legacy boundary

The legacy repository `Uwohali/legacy-hearth-prometheus-whitepaper-pre-v1.1` may be referenced only as archive/provenance.

Permitted label: **ARCHIVE · SUPERSEDED · PRE-v1.1 DEVELOPMENT LINE**

Historical pre-v1.1 development materials are preserved for lineage, provenance and verification history. They do not govern the active canonical line unless explicitly reintroduced through the signed canonical release process.

## 9. Form-to-resource routing

Recommended routing:

- Early Access -> `/resources/`
- Project Prompt -> Runner / project context resource
- Pilot Site -> `/resources/#evidence`
- Science Dossier -> `/resources/#evidence`
- Independent Reviewer -> `/resources/#governance`
- Builder Access -> builder onboarding only after payment confirmation
- Pilot Access -> pilot-preparation onboarding only after payment confirmation
- Consortium Access -> `/resources/#institutional` plus qualification workflow

Form submission is not customer status. Payment is not evidence admission, review approval, pilot admission or consortium acceptance.

## 10. Claims firewall for website copy

- Architecture is not evidence.
- Relational significance is not verified performance.
- External literature is not Prometheus site validation.
- AI, runtime and dashboards are assistive and non-authoritative.
- PRU is an analytical interface, not a right.
- RAP aggregation is not liquidity or market readiness.
- Tokens do not create investor rights by themselves.
- Legal wrappers, not dashboards or tokens, create enforceable rights.
- The lowest unresolved gate controls the strongest permitted claim.

## 11. Required public QA checks

Before publishing or republishing, check for obsolete mailto CTAs, raw CTA headings, HubSpot implementation placeholders, sample cards, obsolete product prices/currencies, raw CSS/HTML fragments, duplicate H1s, obsolete navigation, claims of real runtime validation/certification/current market readiness/validated site performance, and any candidate or legacy document presented as current canon.

## 12. Operating conclusion

The public website should function as a claims-governed evidence and qualification surface, not merely as a marketing funnel.

Public clarity is part of the Prometheus control system: it prevents premature claims, preserves canon hierarchy, protects scientific review, and keeps commercial access separate from empirical, legal and institutional admission gates.
