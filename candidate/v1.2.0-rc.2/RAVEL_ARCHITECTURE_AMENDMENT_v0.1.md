# PROMETHEUS RAVEL Architecture Amendment v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** CANDIDATE / NOT RATIFIED / NOT SIGNED  
**Parent candidate:** `v1.2.0-rc.2`  
**Signed authority in force:** `v1.1.2-genesis`

## Purpose

This amendment evolves the existing Ravel risk-signalling concept into **PROMETHEUS RAVEL — Risk Allocation, Vulnerability, Exposure & Loss**, a candidate quantitative risk-intelligence, shadow-underwriting and loss-allocation layer. It does not alter the non-authority invariant, does not create an investment product, and does not admit any insurance, credit or capital claim.

The deployment problem solved is specific: the current risk architecture can signal fragility but does not yet reconcile (a) the economic loss generated by an asset, (b) genuine loss reduction, (c) contractual allocation/transfer, and (d) the ultimate economic bearer of loss. This amendment therefore replaces no ecological, legal or governance gate and creates no new maturity ladder.

## Canonical placement

[
OHE \rightarrow MRV \rightarrow PRU \rightarrow RAVEL \rightarrow RAP \rightarrow legal\ wrapper \rightarrow capital
]

Interpretation:

- **PRU** represents verified regenerative performance under methodology and gates.
- **RAVEL** evaluates how hazards, vulnerability, finance and verified mitigations change loss distributions, and how losses are allocated.
- **RAP** aggregates eligible units for portfolio analysis.
- **PRMF / governance** owns risk appetite, escalation and decisions.
- **Law** creates enforceable consequences.
- **AI/runtime** remain assistive and non-authoritative.

## Constitutional risk invariants

1. **Loss reduction is not loss allocation.**
2. **No risk disappears through representation.**
3. **No regenerative risk credit without causal evidence.**
4. **No transfer is effective merely because a contract or SPV exists.**
5. **Affiliated entities do not create independent diversification by legal form alone.**
6. **RAVEL signals and calculates; governance interprets; law structures consequences.**
7. **The lowest unresolved gate controls every capital-, insurance- and market-facing claim.**

## Two-ledger architecture

### A. Loss Generation Ledger

Models physical/economic loss before allocation:

[
L^0_j(\omega)=\mathcal{L}(H_j,E_j,V_j,F_j)
]

where (H)=hazard, (E)=exposure, (V)=vulnerability and (F)=financial/operational state.

Verified regenerative evidence may modify vulnerability only after a declared causal model, comparator, uncertainty range and gate.

### B. Risk Allocation Ledger

Applies the loss waterfall across economic bearers, including:

- sponsor equity / first loss;
- reserves;
- subordinated/mezzanine capital;
- senior debt;
- guarantees;
- insurance;
- reinsurance;
- retrocession/capital markets;
- residual retained risk.

For a fully declared system boundary:

[
\sum_b L_b(\omega) \approx L^{mitigated}_{system}(\omega)+Friction(\omega)
]

Transfers between in-boundary parties reconcile rather than disappear.

## Candidate metrics

- **EL** — Expected Loss.
- **ES** — Expected Shortfall at declared confidence.
- **PPCI** — Probability of Permanent Capital Impairment.
- **RRΔ** — Regenerative Risk Delta versus matched baseline.
- **RTE** — Risk Transfer Effectiveness after counterparty/basis/legal/recapture/wrong-way risk.
- **URBR** — Ultimate Risk Bearer Registry.
- **URBC** — Ultimate Risk Bearer Concentration, measured on economic groups where appropriate.
- **RecoveryTime** — scenario-defined recovery distribution.
- **Confidence** — evidence/model confidence disclosed separately from outcome.

Preferred output is a **RAVEL State Vector**, not one scalar rating.

## VRRC boundary

**VRRC — Verified Regenerative Risk Credit** is a future external-underwriting recognition concept.

Current rule:

[
VRRC=0
]

for every capital-, credit-, insurance- or market-facing use until all of the following close:

1. causal evidence gate;
2. reproducible model/calibration gate;
3. independent scientific/actuarial review;
4. legal/admissibility gate;
5. independent insurer/underwriter recognition for the specific use.

A PROMETHEUS model output cannot self-create an insurance discount or lower capital requirement.

## Regenerative Brake

RAVEL may produce Green / Yellow / Orange / Red review signals against a versioned risk appetite. A signal is not a covenant event, credit decision, investment instruction or legal consequence.

- Green — ordinary monitoring.
- Yellow — documented mitigation and owner.
- Orange — mandatory review / restrict affected new capital-facing claims.
- Red — immediate governance review; affected advancement may pause.

Any consequence follows PRMF/governance and valid legal instruments.

## Pilot status

RAVEL enters the Sicily Genesis programme in **Shadow Underwriting Mode** only.

The 12-month G-MEP may collect precursor variables and test evidence plumbing. It does not validate the portfolio-level R1 risk-compression hypothesis. R1 remains multi-site/multi-year and T1 long-horizon.

## Non-goals

RAVEL is not:

- a credit rating agency;
- an insurer or reinsurer;
- a guarantee;
- an autonomous underwriting authority;
- a token-pricing system;
- a substitute for actuarial, legal, scientific or financial diligence;
- proof that regenerative management reduces risk.
