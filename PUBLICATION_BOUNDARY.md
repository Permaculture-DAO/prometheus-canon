# Publication Boundary

This repository is publicly visible for inspection, verification, provenance,
and defensive publication.

Public visibility does not:

- create an offer, security, token, asset, land right, governance right, or
  cash-flow right;
- establish empirical, legal, market, financial, or production validation;
- supersede the signed canonical release identified in `README.md`;
- grant rights beyond the copyright and license notices distributed with the
  relevant files;
- convert drafts, amendments, staging files, runtime output, or AI-generated
  material into canonical authority.

The lowest unresolved evidence, governance, legal, or market gate controls the
permitted claim.
