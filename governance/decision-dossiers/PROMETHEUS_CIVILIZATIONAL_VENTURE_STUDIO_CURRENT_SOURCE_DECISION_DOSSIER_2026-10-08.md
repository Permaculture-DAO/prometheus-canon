## 5. Aeterna / Ravel risk architecture

Aeterna defines the controlled risk methodology. Ravel operationalises approved methods into Risk Objects, ledgers, stress tests and decision-support outputs.

Mandatory chain:

`Hazard → Trigger → Exposure → Vulnerability → Direct Loss → Secondary Loss → Frequency/Probability where defensible → Severity → Dependence → Recovery → Prevention → Mitigation → Diversification → Absorption → Transfer → Retention → Residual Risk → Unmodelled Risk → Ultimate Risk Bearer`.

Mandatory ledgers:

1. **Loss Generation Ledger** — where and why loss is generated.
2. **Risk Allocation Ledger** — who absorbs, finances, transfers or externalises that loss.

`LOSS REDUCTION != LOSS ALLOCATION`.  
`TRANSFERRED RISK != ELIMINATED RISK`.

If frequency or severity cannot be defended, use **NR — NOT RELIABLY RATEABLE** rather than fabricated precision.
