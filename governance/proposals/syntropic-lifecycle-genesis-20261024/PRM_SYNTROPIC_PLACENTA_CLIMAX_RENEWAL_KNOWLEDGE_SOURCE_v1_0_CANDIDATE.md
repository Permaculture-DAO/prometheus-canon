## 1. Scope and category boundaries

- Plant succession ecology provides inspiration, **not evidence that an organization follows ecology**. Ecological *placenta*, *secondary*, *climax* refer to design/life-cycle concepts whose application to digital governance and enterprises is analogical.
- Do not treat progression as one-way, timed by default, inevitable, or success-guaranteeing. Various functional guilds may coexist; setbacks, disturbance, human choice, legal duties and resource limits are explicit.
- Do not fuse conceptual stages with the CANON maturity ladder, PRU/RAP statuses, legal rights, capital permissions or TRBK. Organisational stages do not confer ecological, legal or economic admission.
- Apply existing source hierarchy and claims discipline. `UNKNOWN != ZERO`, `MODEL != OBSERVATION`, `RISK TRANSFER != RISK ELIMINATION`, `BIOMIMICRY != SCIENTIFIC VALIDATION`, `AI != COMPETENT HUMAN AUTHORITY`.
- This text is **source content proposed for the existing catalog**, not a demand for a new named normative subsystem. No registration/merge/deployment performed by drafting.

## 3. Candidate stages and falsifiers

| Stage | Operational hypothesis | Minimum observable evidence | Progression gate | Failure / pruning trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Placenta / foundational niche** | A small protected operating envelope allows inexpensive external problem discovery, baseline design, explicit rights and a minimal useful service | dated stakeholder problem/consent, source provenance, actual effort/spend and deliverable | at least one scoped external use/review of a useful artifact, no major rights/safety blocker | documentation without user or ecological decision utility; dominant founder/infrastructure dependency |
| **Pioneer / colonization** | Initial services and independent stewards can operate without fictitious demand, and failures are recorded | legitimate customer/field use, independent feedback, cost logs, tested rollback | repeatable use cases with audited limits | only internal praise; hidden externalities; unresolved quality failures |
| **Secondary / accumulation** | Evidence, competencies, working capital and operating buffers improve repeatability | baseline/comparator and uncertainty for field claims; incident log, backups, cash/reserves evidence for organization | independently replayable evidence and feasible continuity | maintenance/documentation overwhelms field benefits; dangerous liquidity or staff concentration |
| **Diversified consortium** | Complementary specialist components outperform isolated alternatives without concealment of correlated failure | cross-module dependency map, independent pilot transfer, composite resource/time log | independently reproduced incremental advantage over competent lean baseline | common-mode failure, over-integration, fragile cross-agency dependencies |
| **Provisional climax / mature resilience** | Operations, custody, correction and learning continue through operator turnover and external disturbance | independent recovery/restore, distributed stewardship, effective rights/remedies, reproducible scientific review | measured stability, acceptable uncertainty and founder-independent continuity | single-person control, inability to recover, impossible maintenance, suppression of dissent |
| **Renewal / new succession** | Unproductive structures are removed and knowledge recycles without destroying provenance | decomission/replacement records, retained hashes, impact of removals | after independent review, verified simpler/repaired alternative works at least as well | repeated failure to retire redundant modules or loss of living-system outcomes |

No stage asserts that nature equals safety, syntropy equals solvency or economic value rises automatically. Stage labels are **not** regulatory or financial classifications.

## 4. Causal and economic falsifiability plan

**Primary comparison:** full biomimetic PROMETHEUS workflow versus a competent, lower-complexity workflow (domain expert + normal GIS/spreadsheet/source citations), performing matched decision tasks on the same evidence packets. Pre-register cases, reviewers, blind scoring when possible, completeness, adverse-error detection, reproducibility, rework, time, total cost, training burden and security/rights incidents. At least one truly independent evaluator must review disagreements; no self-selected positive examples only.

**Candidate falsification:** if the full workflow has materially higher life-cycle cost/complexity and no pre-specified, reliable improvement in decision utility, reproducibility, adverse-risk detection or ecological delivery, **SIMPLIFY/RETIRE** relevant elements. A serious rights/privacy/security breach => HOLD regardless of favorable scores. A claim of autonomous/no-founder operation must be stress-tested against founder absence, vendor loss, off-host restore and loss of one critical steward; a successful simulation cannot be called externally verified continuity.

**Important:** numerical thresholds and task counts must be determined before testing by the responsible operators and independent reviewers, grounded in measurement variation and operational constraints. Do not optimize thresholds using observed results.
