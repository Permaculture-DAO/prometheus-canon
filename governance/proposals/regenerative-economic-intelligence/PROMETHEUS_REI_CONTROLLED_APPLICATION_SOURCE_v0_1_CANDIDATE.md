## 2. Normative application invariants

1. `UNKNOWN != ZERO`. Missing, stale, contradictory, unauthenticated and structurally invalid evidence remain visibly distinct.
2. `LOSS_REDUCTION != LOSS_ALLOCATION`; `TRANSFERRED_RISK != ELIMINATED_RISK`; insurance or a transfer shifts incidence but does not prove the physical system loss disappeared.
3. `P/H/Q -> D verification -> human/domain authority -> permissible action`; quantum-ready, never quantum-dependent. LLM reasoning and scenario probabilities cannot confer permissions.
4. Regenerative activities and market prices may diverge. No ecological uplift, land value, carbon credit or financial premium shall be inferred from ecological intent or from a correlated observation.
5. Any material rights breach, environmental externality, unallocated loss or compromised evidence shall not be compensated for by a positive score in another dimension.
6. Legal ownership, tenant rights, water and land-use authorisations, insurance binding, investment approval and capital release exist only through competent legal/professional acts, not this app.
7. Hash agreement proves declared-snapshot consistency, NOT source custody/authenticity, calibration, independent reviewer competence or real-world fact.
8. Human recipients of costs and benefits, community rights, fiscal leakage, related-party conflicts and nonhuman ecosystem burdens must not be invisibly excluded from an extractive-investment comparison.
9. Evidence claims shall preserve measurement unit, spatial/temporal boundary, as-of and provenance; baseline != counterfactual; an unmeasured effect is not a zero effect.
10. The application shall not self-select its own governing Source ID/authority, or change an existing AETERNA/RAVEL/Civilizational/Canon source or real runtime path.

## 3. Typed claim and decision domain

Minimum cross-sector dossier identity: `project_id`, `sector`, declared assessment `as_of`, versioned schema, explicit source dependencies and evidence references. Project/site identity must not be silently merged across locations or jurisdictions.

Candidate dimensions are six disjoint **inventory categories**, not commensurable currencies: `FINANCIAL`, `PRODUCTIVE`, `ECOLOGICAL`, `SOCIAL`, `GOVERNANCE`, `RISK`. Each claim records its method reference, evidence references, epistemic state, measurement value/unit if any, baseline evidence, and counterfactual evidence where applicable.

Epistemic states:
- `HYPOTHESIS`: unverified claim for research, not a measured fact.
- `OBSERVATION_CANDIDATE`: claimed measurement, still awaiting custody and competent independent verification.
- `CAUSAL_CANDIDATE`: asserted contribution requiring explicit identification strategy, confounder analysis, baseline/counterfactual and approved method.
- `VALUATION_CANDIDATE`: asserted price or monetary effect requiring independent lawful appraisal and accepted professional methodology.

A claim changing material permissions cannot pass solely because it is self-consistent. The first engineering contract uses `HOLD` for proposed causal/valuation claims and for P/H/Q claim modes until a separate D verification boundary and qualified reviewers are built. A declared `OBSERVATION_CANDIDATE` must not be displayed as a verified observation.

Sectors: `REAL_ESTATE`, `AGRICULTURE`, `ENERGY`, `INFRASTRUCTURE`. The real-estate implementation composes existing **non-authoritative** land-readiness and territorial safeguards. The other sectors are in `HOLD: domain_adapter:not_implemented` until independently reviewed source, data contract and tests exist. Sector recognition alone is not readiness.

## 5. AETERNA / RAVEL contract: prevention and allocation are two different ledgers

REI must not redefine `ScenarioLoss`, RAVEL risk objects, actuarial calibration or economic-group aggregation. It may reference independent risk methodology and RAVEL outputs by pinned version and verified digests once a controlled integration exists.

The **Loss Generation Ledger** addresses physical/economic loss event, cause/hazard, trigger, exposure, vulnerability, gross loss, *actual* prevention or loss reduction, residual system loss, scenario assumptions and evidence. The **Risk Allocation Ledger** addresses how that remaining loss is borne: retained equity, debt, insurer, guarantee, fiscal recipient, worker, local resident, supplier, ecosystem or explicitly `UNALLOCATED/UNKNOWN`.

The concept of `ultimate_risk_bearer` requires identifiable real-world bearer and instrument evidence, not a claimant-provided name. The REI v0.1 contract only checks declared IDs and string consistency; it does **not** verify ledger documents, transfer performance, policy enforceability, or loss conservation mathematically.

Before substantive use, a separate reviewed RAVEL adapter must explicitly assess:
- `gross_system_loss - verified_prevention = residual_system_loss` (only for compatible units, horizon and scenario definition);
- `sum(bearer_allocated_losses) + unallocated_loss = residual_system_loss`;
- actual transfer `in` and `out` must conserve *system* loss rather than apparently eliminate it;
- stale/unknown probabilities and unmodelled loss must remain `NR` / explicit unknown;
- tail risk and common-shock concentration must be reviewed under AETERNA, not REI;
- independent evidence needed for insurer solvency, policy scope, financial/insurer/legal transfer and claims payment.

The adapter cannot grant policy, debt, capital, valuation or regulatory authority. Preserving explanatory losses does not prove the insured risk is economically contained.

## 6. Anti-extraction and territorial value safeguards

REI is meant to test whether an investment funds verifiable productive/maintenance improvements without simply accelerating scarcity rent or shifting loss to nonconsenting parties. It must document, separately: pre-existing land/asset value, owner improvements, public infrastructure effects, scarcity rents, legitimate development profit, community benefits, unearned gains, ecosystem condition and loss bearers. It shall not assign all price appreciation to ecological regeneration.

Candidate governance options (not universally required or automatically sufficient): Community Land Trust, shared-equity, ground lease, public-interest covenants, cooperative stewardship, land banks, land value tax where legally applicable, local-benefit contracts. The legal enforceability and equity of each option need local independent review. At the application level, no mechanism can substitute for executed instruments.

Comparison with conventional due diligence, Propy-style transaction platforms, tokenized asset systems (including RealT/RMM) and qualified local professional services must distinguish actual operational outcomes and jurisdiction-specific duties. Tokenized exposure does not automatically equal legal title or ecological quality. TRBK remains behind separate regulatory/technical gates.

## 8. Falsifiability

H1: Fixed requests replay to byte-identical output and input digest independent of row ordering. A single non-blocked unknown/material forgery invalidates the implementation claims.

H2: A matched, audited generation/allocation ledger proves no residual loss disappeared through mere transfer. Any unreconciled significant loss mandates HOLD.

H3: Real-world ecological impact is identified with pre-registered method, time-series baseline, comparison/counterfactual where justified, controls, measurement uncertainty, leakage, persistence and competent domain evidence. A single observed number is not proof of causal regeneration.

H4: External reviewers find better quality/cost than qualified incumbent alternatives. No improvement => simplify or retire adapter; do not force a platform business case.

H5: Quantum or quantum-inspired optimisers must outperform strong classical baselines under equivalent objective and constraints with reproducible cost/performance metrics; otherwise Q stays SHADOW.

H6: Distributional, rights and environmental safeguards reveal who ultimately bears non-financial harms. An externalised burden fails the anti-extraction claim regardless of sponsor returns.

H7: Legal and operational demand for an asset-light evidence-readiness service is demonstrated by real buyers/payments and authorised contracts; pure narrative is not a sale.
