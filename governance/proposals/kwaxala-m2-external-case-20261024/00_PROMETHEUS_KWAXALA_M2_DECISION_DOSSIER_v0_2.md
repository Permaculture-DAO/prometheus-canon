## Quantitative logic that must be enforced

For project `p`, vintage `v`, and buyer `b`:
- `Issued_tCO2e[p,v]` comes only from an independently identified **registry record** after validation/verification and issuance; no interpolation from conservation area.
- `DeliverableForSale[p,v] = Issued - Retired - Cancelled - AlreadyTransferred - ReservedByContract` when all serial disjointness and rights checks pass; otherwise state **UNKNOWN**, not zero.
- `NetCollectedCash[p,t] = BankClearedReceipts - ActualBankOutflows` after segregating restricted grants, escrow and tax liabilities. Non-cash ecosystem gains **never** enter cash at par.
- `AvailableForDistribution = max(0, CashAfterLegallyDueObligationsAndValidReservations)`. `FundingGap = max(0, -CashAfterLegallyDueObligationsAndValidReservations)` remains visible, never recast as no-loss.
- No distribution percentage exists without executed agreements and a verified actual-bank payment trail.
- `AssetValue` must separately state fair value (if permitted), book value, **net realizable collateral**, liquidity and rights expiry; no perpetuity where contract has term/termination risk.
- For risk, show: generation mechanism, mitigation, external transfer legal perfection, retained amount, **ultimate liable/bearer**, common-cause/correlation, maximum foreseeable ecological externality and uncertainty.

## Scientific research tests — independent controls

**ECO-1 legal-and-carbon additionality:** identify dated counterfactual land management and legal baseline at project start; review under actually applicable protocol, treatment boundaries, leakage, risk-of-reversal, double counts. **Falsifier:** no defensible incremental climate benefit after 2024 protection, or rights/serial mismatch. Outcome = not credit-admissible; legal protection remains real.

**ECO-2 ecosystem outcomes:** repeated field plots with comparator matched by forest type/age/land-use history and climate, *true independent replication*; prereg native diversity, water-cycle outcome, stand structure, soil carbon, data QA/missingness. **Falsifier:** null/adverse effects, effects explained by confounders, or insufficient sample power. No universal nature score.

**ECO-3 Centree-type metric:** define `Z = [habitat structure, native richness, soil/water, disturbance sensitivity, ecological connectivity]`, with measurement and uncertainty for each feature. Compare predictive performance out-of-region to baseline of GIS/NDVI/stand age and qualified ecologist; no arbitrary sum or tCO2e conversion. **Falsifier:** fails calibration, robustness, transfer or adds more complexity/cost than its measurable gain. No claim of reproducing proprietary Centree formula.

**GOV-1 genuine community autonomy:** trace voting/consent/control, debt security, transfer, insolvency, default and exit to actual contracts; role-play adverse court-enforceable outcomes with local counsel. **Falsifier:** rights can be unintentionally alienated, collateralized or diluted without competent community consent.

**REI-1 cash and fairness:** independently reconcile site-specific benefit allocation, fund pooling, fees, bank receipts and paid distributions. **Falsifier:** actual cash flows do not reconcile to asserted waterfall or marketing percentages after cost and taxes.

**RAVEL-1 loss / diversification:** stress carbon price collapse, zero issuance, regional wildfire, FX, reserve shortfall, law change, fund insolvency, local stewardship interruption and buyer default across correlated sites. **Falsifier:** pooling concentrates exposures or risk is shifted to local partners without informed consent and protective compensation.
