# 0. EXECUTIVE DECISION

## 0.1 One-sentence definition

The Regenerative Territorial Capital Operating System (RTC-OS) is a proposed PROMETHEUS application capability for preparing, monitoring and independently reviewing territorial regeneration decisions through a single traceable sequence connecting real places and their stewards to evidence, intervention, risk, enforceable rights and legally permissible capital pathways.

## 0.2 Purpose

RTC-OS must answer seven distinct questions without collapsing them:

1. WHAT EXISTS? What physical, ecological, social, infrastructural, cultural, legal and financial reality is observed within the defined territorial boundary?
2. WHAT CAN CHANGE? What regenerative interventions are technically plausible, lawful and locally acceptable?
3. WHAT CAN BE KNOWN? What is actually observed, verified, reproducible and causally attributable rather than merely hypothesised?
4. WHAT CAN FAIL? What losses may arise, who is exposed, which controls change loss generation, which contracts change loss allocation, and which loss remains?
5. WHO HAS RIGHTS? Who may use, govern, steward, finance, derive benefits from or exit the intervention, under what enforceable instruments?
6. WHO PAYS AND WHY? Is there a real buyer, payer, funder or public-interest mandate, and can the project survive without speculative revenue assumptions?
7. WHAT MAY BE CLAIMED OR FINANCED NOW? Which evidence, maturity, legal and market gates have actually closed, and which human/domain authority may act?

The primary product is **decision integrity**, not automatically financing, higher land price, ecological certification, tokenisation or scale.

## 0.3 Decision on architecture

**DESIGN GO** — Compile RTC-OS as a new application profile under PRM-CIV-APP-006.

**IMPLEMENTATION CONDITIONAL GO** — Permit schema design, non-capital-facing shadow computations, synthetic examples, software testing, stakeholder research and authorized pilot reconnaissance.

**HOLD** — Production assertions of independently validated ecological improvement, certified land value, insurable risk compression, legal rights, investor-readiness, financeable asset class or proven TOPOS partnership.

**NO-GO** — Any software, token, model, narrative or scoring rule bypassing source authority, verified evidence, independent review, lawful instruments and human capital authority.

## 0.4 Novelty and complexity budget

RTC-OS is a **domain configuration, service composition and deliverable package** of existing PROMETHEUS core facilities: Source Registry, Evidence Spine, MRV, Claims Register, Gate Engine, PRU/RAP where applicable, Aeterna methodology, Ravel operations, Quant Kernel, Rights Map and human decision ledger.

It does not add a second ontology of ecological truth, independent underwriting standard, parallel investment approval, token economy or constitutional hierarchy. Before adding a module, prove its necessity and its advantage over a simpler conventional diligence workflow.

## 1.5 Four nested scales: Universal Ethics → Global Principles → Bioregional Strategies → Local Solutions

**Source of concept:** the user-provided concentric-circle diagram reads, from outer to inner level, “Ethics are Universal”, “Principles are Global”, “Strategies are Bioregional” and “Solutions are Local”. RTC-OS integrates this as an **operational scoping discipline**, not as empirical proof of universal moral consensus, a new constitutional hierarchy, or a claim to political jurisdiction over local people.

| Scope | RTC-OS interpretation | Mandatory control | Proposed record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E — Universal ethics** | Design commitments to dignity, non-extraction, voluntary informed participation, fairness, ecological integrity, risk allocation and intergenerational stewardship | Coercion, displacement, concealed harms, capture, and unreviewed rights conflicts cannot be offset by commercial benefits; Canon and applicable law continue to govern | EthicalBoundaryAssessment |
| **P — Global principles** | Portable procedures: evidence before value, source provenance, falsifiability, uncertainty disclosure, no-double-counting, risk-owner mapping, reproducibility and distributed human authority | A portable method is not a portable empirical result. | **B — Bioregional strategies** | Place-sensitive strategy shaped by watershed, soils, climate, ecology, food systems, cultural history, institutions, disturbance, operating capability and stewardship | Relevant reference-class evidence, comparator, rights, hazards and local legitimacy must be examined; untested transfer is EXPLORATORY, not proven | BioregionalStrategyProfile |
| **L — Local solutions** | Site-specific species, planting, water design, contracting, labour, budgets, operations, maintenance and adaptive monitoring | Actual site permission, baseline, legal water/title, steward accountability, independent review where material, Aeterna/Ravel risk and valid financial/legal wrappers | LocalSolutionPlan |

**Reading rule:** ethics delimit acceptable goals; principles delimit valid decision and evidence procedures; bioregional strategies determine which context-appropriate pathways are worth testing; local solutions execute testable interventions. Locally observed outcomes and dissent feed **upwards** to improve strategy and methods, but do not automatically rewrite the Canon or convert a project result into a universal claim.

### 1.5.1 Non-compensating four-scale decision vector

Record separate E, P, B, L states for each material decision:

- E_state: ethical/rights compatibility — PASSED, HOLD, REJECT, UNKNOWN.
- P_state: methodological and claims integrity — PASSED, HOLD, REJECT, UNKNOWN.
- B_state: bioregional contextual applicability — PASSED, EXPLORATORY, HOLD, UNKNOWN.
- L_state: permitted and feasible local implementation — PASSED, HOLD, REJECT, UNKNOWN.

This vector is **not a composite investment score**. A productive local intervention cannot compensate for coercion, a verified global generalisation cannot substitute for a local baseline or water right, and a lawful site does not guarantee ecological success. Even if all four scales are passed, the existing G0–G7 gates, PRU/RAP maturity, legal instrument, insurance and capital authority remain separately required.

### 1.5.2 Bioregional plurality, feedback and lawful authority

A single site may belong to multiple useful territorial frames (watershed, agroclimatic zone, ecoregion, administrative area or culturally defined food system). Preserve overlapping maps, source IDs, boundary uncertainty and conflicts instead of claiming one universal bioregional geometry.

“Universal ethics” is an explicitly declared PROMETHEUS design commitment, **not** a claim that all communities share identical ethics. Respect valid Indigenous/customary and statutory rights, privacy, cultural knowledge, safeguarding, public resources and local participation. No “bioregional” label automatically creates jurisdiction, ownership, representation, legal authority or investment rights.

A material E/P/B/L inconsistency is a SCALE_CONFLICT. Its record carries site_id, source_ids, affected claims, reviewer, materiality, conflicting observations, dissent, remediation and expiry. An unresolved material conflict forces HOLD on the affected claims and capital-facing decisions.

### 1.5.3 Application composition, without parallel architecture

Extend existing PROMETHEUS objects by reference:

- TerritoryProfile links one or more BioregionalStrategyProfile records with boundaries, evidence and confidence.
- TerritorialDeterminationDossier includes E/P/B/L gate vector, baseline, do-nothing alternative and material conflicts.
- InterventionPlan / LocalSolutionPlan includes steward, rights, local ecological context, budget, management, expected failures and stop conditions.
- Evidence Spine and Claims Register remain the controls on observation, method, causal inference, dissemination and cross-scale generalisation.
- Aeterna methods and Ravel operations record physical and financial risks, transfer terms and ultimate bearers including communities and ecosystems.
- Deterministic Verification Boundary applies to all P/H/Q risk and recommendation outputs; human/domain authority remains external.

### 1.5.4 Illustrative Sicilian temperate OHE, not observed proof

At a prospective Sicilian syntropic food-forest pilot: E demands dignified voluntary stewardship, transparent community impacts and non-extractive water use; P demands time-stamped soil/water baselines, comparable control, preregistered outcomes and reproducible review; B adapts the strategy to Mediterranean drought, soils, water balance and landscape disturbance; L specifies lawfully permitted species, local spacing, water-use design, maintenance, labour and financing obligations. **RTC-OS design commitment:** universally bounded ethical aims, globally consistent procedural principles, bioregionally adaptive strategy, and locally accountable solutions — with evidence, applicable law, safeguarding and legitimate human rights never bypassed.

# 2. DEFINITION OF TERRITORY, SYSTEM AND USER

## 2.1 Unit of analysis

The primary decision unit is:

**Territorial System × Intervention × Steward/Operator × Legal Perimeter × Financing Perimeter × Time Horizon × Counterfactual**.

A Territory is a bounded biophysical and human system; it is not reducible to a parcel, priced asset or token. A Site may be a particular tract of land within a larger watershed, neighbourhood, municipality or bioregion. Geographic boundaries for ecology, hydrology, legal title, beneficiaries and financial accounts need not coincide; all are independently declared.

Territorial State is a typed vector with at least six distinct records:

- physical/ecological state;
- evidence and causal-inference state;
- stewardship, social legitimacy and public-value state;
- operating/economic state;
- legal-rights and governance state;
- financial risk, capital and liquidity state.

No composite score may substitute for an unresolved mandatory state.

## 2.2 Representative applications, not presumed deployments

Potential domain profiles include regenerative food forests and agroforestry; watershed and soil rehabilitation; degraded land conversion; community/municipal resilience infrastructure; school food forests subject to safeguarding; hospitality/education sites; and publicly mandated natural infrastructure.

Each profile needs its own buyer/payer, legal perimeter, evidence plan, intervention alternatives and objective. No cross-domain universality is inferred from the profile name.

## 2.3 Users and authorities

- **Local stewards/operators:** authorised field operations, upkeep, incident reporting and local knowledge.
- **Property/rights holders and communities:** legally bounded rights and participation; not automatically identical to financial investors.
- **Territorial analysts/designers:** reconnaissance, evidence-gathering and intervention proposals; no independent certification by default.
- **PROMETHEUS technology operators:** maintain reproducible records and non-authoritative diagnostics.
- **Scientific/MRV reviewers:** independent review within competence and declared scope.
- **Independent appraisers/valuers:** market valuation and attribution opinions, separate from sponsor/engineer incentives.
- **Legal counsel and public authorities:** determine rights, permits, obligations and lawful finance structures.
- **Insurers/underwriters:** bind coverage only through their own decision process.
- **Investment/credit/public-finance committees:** decide legally permissible capital uses; no software substitutes for their powers.
- **Potential external partners such as TOPOS:** may provide territorial intelligence or design services only under written arrangements, a conflict-of-interest map and validated output acceptance.

An organisation may hold multiple roles only when lawful conflict safeguards and review independence remain credible and documented. The system does not assume a signed agreement with TOPOS.

# 4. TERRITORIAL INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT

## 4.1 Minimum layers

**Place intelligence** is a source-linked synthesis of biophysical, socio-ecological, civic and financial constraints. The intake must support, subject to permissions:

1. landform/geology/slope and soil functions;
2. seasonal and long-horizon water balances, access and water-rights;
3. natural hazards including drought, fire, flood, heat and relevant dependencies;
4. vegetation, habitat connectivity, biodiversity and ecological succession;
5. energy and critical infrastructure dependencies;
6. food-system capacity, agricultural labour and input dependency;
7. parcel/title/tenure/use rights and planning/permits;
8. public/private service infrastructure and relevant institutional arrangements;
9. local steward capability, community consent and grievance routes;
10. baseline costs, cash flows, existing demands and potential payers;
11. ecological/cultural knowledge with appropriate consent, rights and attribution;
12. data gaps, conflicting sources and confidence limits.

Each layer is evidence-status tagged. Where location geometry reveals sensitive ecology, children or vulnerable individuals, release geospatial data only at a safe granularity and with lawful access controls.

## 4.2 Required comparators

The Territorial Determination Dossier must compare:
- **No action / business as usual**, including maintenance and likely deterioration assumptions;
- **Minimum viable low-complexity intervention**;
- **Regenerative design candidate**;
- **Alternative implementation or sequencing**, where plausible.

Alternatives must disclose land/water/rights constraints, establishment and recurring OPEX, operator labour, time to biological maturity, liquidity needs and risks. A proposal may result in **DO NOT DEVELOP** or **RESTORE WITHOUT FINANCIALISATION**.

## 4.3 Territorial Determination Dossier (TDD)

The minimum TDD contains:

**Identity:** territory/site identifiers, boundary geometries, scale and decision horizon.  
**Authority:** initiating party, site access permission, rights/mandate status, named reviewer and dissent.  
**Reality:** geolocated/source-bound baseline, source quality and unresolved unknowns.  
**Design:** intervention options, causal hypotheses, implementation phasing and maintenance.  
**Consequences:** distribution of ecological, fiscal, community and financial effects, both positive and adverse.  
**Risks:** hazards, vulnerabilities, gross/direct/secondary loss and ultimate risk bearers.  
**Economics:** viable payer, CAPEX/OPEX, labour, cash flow, scenario downside; excluded speculative uplifts.  
**Decision:** eligible option, HOLD/REDESIGN/REJECT, required field evidence, expiry and next gate.  
**Reproducibility:** source IDs, method versions, calculation parameters, data hashes and reviewer trail.

The TDD is not a title deed, appraisal, credit approval, independent certification, environmental impact assessment or securities offering.

# 5. EVIDENCE, CLAIMS AND SCIENTIFIC FALSIFIABILITY

## 5.1 Evidence Spine binding

Every material observation must link to an Evidence Spine record containing, as appropriate: evidence ID, source agent, site ID, geospatial scope, timestamp, method ID/version, units, raw value, calibration, transformations, method/data hashes, missingness, anomaly state, reviewer, related claim/risk IDs and current evidence status.

Retain the active PRM-ARCH-DET-002 evidence-state progression:

**RAW → IDENTIFIED → PROVENANCE_BOUND → QA_QC_CHECKED → REVIEWABLE → VERIFIED_INDICATOR_CANDIDATE → ADMISSIBLE or REJECTED.**

No status is automatically inherited across PRU, Ravel, RAP, legal-use or public-facing claims.

## 5.2 Pre-registration before confirmatory analysis

For each material intended improvement, freeze before confirmatory evaluation: unit of analysis, target, baseline, design/management change, comparison approach, expected effect and direction, minimum observation horizon, missing-data rule, confounders, outcome metric, estimator, uncertainty method, test of failure and review responsibility.

The existing PROMETHEUS scientific H1–H5 are reused only within their actual scope; new territorial application hypotheses must have independent IDs and cannot imply that syntropic results generalise to every region or industry.

## 5.3 Claim policy

A displayed observation is not a verified outcome. A verified outcome is not necessarily causally attributable. Causal ecological outcome does not automatically establish market price or avoided financial loss. Avoided financial loss does not automatically mean an insurance policy will price it. A publicly described outcome does not create legal rights.

No report, investor material, sales page, LLM response or API consumer may use claims stronger than the current Claims Register and weakest material gate permit.

# 6. Aeterna / Ravel — Risk Control Plane

## 6.1 Method roles

**Aeterna:** controlled actuarial, insurance, reinsurance, financial risk and capital protection **methodology**.  
**Ravel:** risk operationalisation and decision-support **application**, in SHADOW mode unless validated for a specified use.

They do not create a new insurance company, binding coverage, published rating, legal opinion, lender approval or scientifically valid probability from text.

## 6.2 Risk object and chain

Each material risk follows the registered chain:

**Hazard → Trigger → Exposure → Vulnerability → Direct Loss → Secondary Loss → Frequency or defensible Probability → Severity → Dependence → Recovery → Prevention → Mitigation → Diversification → Absorption → Transfer → Retention → Residual Risk → Unmodelled Risk → Ultimate Risk Bearer.**

Risk Object fields minimally include risk/site IDs, evidence and claim references, risk category, hazard, trigger, direct/secondary losses, horizon, frequency/severity/dependence when defensible, controls, transfer IDs, retained/residual/unmodelled risk, confidence and provenance, model version, owner, reviewer and review frequency.

If frequency/severity cannot be defended, mark **NR — NOT RELIABLY RATEABLE** and report stress scenarios without invented probabilities.

## 6.3 Two ledgers, not one score

**Loss Generation Ledger:** where the loss arises, physical/economic mechanism, gross severity, time to recovery and evidence.

**Risk Allocation Ledger:** prevented, mitigated, insured, guaranteed, hedged, absorbed, publicly borne, externally imposed and retained losses, keyed to loss event IDs and ultimate bearers.

Both must reconcile monetary units and time horizons; loss shifted to workforce, public budget, community, ecosystems or future generations remains recorded as transferred/externalised impact, not eliminated economic damage.

## 6.4 Applicable measures and boundaries

Depending on demonstrably adequate data, Aeterna/Ravel may generate expected loss, scenarios, VaR/ES, cash-flow-at-risk, debt capacity and liquidity stress, PD/LGD/EAD, exposure concentration, tail dependence, recovery time, reserves, risk-transfer recoverability and PPCI. The measures are diagnostic and use the controlling methodology's definitions, calibration and limitations; this application does not redefine them.

A scenario without defensible probabilities yields a loss range and decisive unknowns, not fictitious percentile confidence. Any economic rationale for insurance must compare retained risk, protection price, exclusions, deductibles, limits, counterparties and timeliness of recovery.

## 6.5 Double solvency and social/ecological externalities

Operational solvency and biological resilience are different properties. Proposed Dual Solvency and RFVL concepts remain **candidate application tests** under Civilization governance; they are not certified covenants, official indicators or proof of insurability.

A material financing risk reduction that worsens uncompensated community or ecosystem loss is not labelled net social risk reduction. Risk must be distributed transparently and lawfully.

# 7. RIGHTS, STEWARDSHIP AND TERRITORIAL NON-EXTRACTION

## 7.1 Rights and Responsibilities Map

At each site, identify and distinguish: title, lease, possession, access, water extraction/use, harvest, improvement rights, community benefit commitments, stewardship duties, governance powers, IP/data ownership, insurance proceeds, cash-flow interests, collateral, lender recourse, insolvency priority, termination, reversion, exit costs and dispute resolution.

A promise in a dashboard or community covenant is not a substitute for enforceability analysis. Public-land programmes and projects involving schools require the corresponding higher-priority safeguards, permissions, child safety and benefit-independence rules.

## 7.2 Territorial capture controls

The value-creation ledger and the distribution ledger must be analytically separable. Explicitly test displacement, coercive land-value capture, loss of access, rent escalation, insecure tenure, water appropriation, dependence on unpaid labour, unequal information, data extraction, price transfer to external financiers, governance concentration and adverse effects on future stewardship.

Financial value that increases while local ecological or public benefit worsens must be reported as a conflicting result, not a regenerative success.

## 7.3 Independent lane

Independence of the scientific reviewer, professional appraiser, assurance provider and jurisdictional counsel is required where their opinion is material. Sponsor, land operator, PROMETHEUS provider, capital allocator and external territorial consultant cannot self-assign each other's independent authority.

Where the same corporate group provides multiple services, related-party conflicts and reviewer selection are disclosed; project claims remain gated until sufficiently independent assurance exists.

# 8. CAPITAL RAILS, CASH FLOW AND LEGAL FIREWALL

## 8.1 Project economic discipline

The base-case analysis must be viable or explicitly labelled non-viable **without** appreciation of TRBK, unverified carbon/biodiversity credits, hypothetical land uplift, speculative entitlements, unfunded grants or uncontracted ecosystem-service payments. Use defensible demand, rights, operator capacity, agricultural/hospitality/service revenue where applicable, CAPEX/OPEX, labour, contingencies, maintenance, debt service and downside liquidity.

The source makes no return guarantee and does not assert every regenerative site should be an investable asset.

## 8.2 Candidate financing channels

Potential capital paths, each independently gated, include philanthropic grants, project development grants, public procurement, municipal budget appropriation, concessional debt, blended finance, project finance, private investment, dedicated special-purpose vehicles, insurance/risk transfer and — only where issuer, governing law, regulatory approvals and investor protections permit — sovereign or sub-sovereign retail debt.

Retail sovereign finance is **a public-debt instrument sold by a valid sovereign issuer**, not a PROMETHEUS utility token or a local SPV pretending to hold sovereign authority. Neither regional nor municipal issuance is presumed lawful for any particular geography. Public finance must disclose repayment source, borrowing powers, procurement, sovereign/municipal credit risk, bondholder rights and retail investor protection. For retail distribution, KYC/AML, custody, suitability/appropriateness where applicable, disclosure, secondary liquidity and consumer protections require separate review.

## 8.3 Capital rails firewall

**PRU:** analytical quality representation, not a right or collateral by default.  
**RAP:** portfolio aggregation, not itself a transferable security, market or fund.  
**TRBK:** separately defined consumptive utility/access proposal, not project-return exposure.  
**Investment exposure:** separate compliant right/security where legally structured.  
**Sovereign/sub-sovereign security:** exclusively issued/authorised under relevant public and securities law.  
**Digital settlement or ledger:** operational record, not source of repayment rights or certification.  
**Insurance contract:** insurer's enforceable obligation subject to trigger, exclusions, deductibles, limits, counterparty and claims terms.

## 8.4 Capital readiness objects and hard blockers

CapitalReadinessDossier contains: completed rights/title/water review; approved intervention and steward contract; baseline and evidence status; audited/reviewable cost model; five-case economics where the parent application requires it; downside runway; Ravel risk and recovery scenarios; two-ledger risk allocation; independent valuation and attribution range; conflicts; partner contracts; actual payer/offtake evidence; legal and tax memo; insurance/contingency; community/exit plan; Claims Register; dissent; committee recommendation with date and expiry.

At land closing, **Civilization v6.2 controls**: preserve existing Land IC G0–G5 plus independent legal, financial, insurance and human approval prerequisites. An algorithmic score cannot override HOLD. Any release of capital must occur outside PROMETHEUS under authorised persons and contracts.

# 9. VALUE, VALUATION AND ADDITIONALITY FIREWALL

Separate four claims:
1. **Land/ecosystem change:** measured physical/ecological outcomes.
2. **Enterprise improvement:** changes in operating income, costs, volatility, resilience, cash flow or maintenance.
3. **Financing improvement:** observed change in borrowability, insurance, covenants, pricing or recovery, with confounders and counterfactual.
4. **Independent market valuation:** appraiser-supported land/instrument value with an attribution range and legal bounds.

Even if land values rise, the increase cannot be entirely attributed to regeneration without defensible counterfactual evidence. Changes in markets, rezoning, infrastructure, subsidies, scarcity, land tenure and buyer composition must be separated.

Where PRU value analysis is eligible, apply **the single controlling Canon valuation expression and its original gates/caps/confidence conditions**. Do not create a second competing formula or claim that a regenerative index directly prices a security.

Avoid unqualified terms such as “verified land value”, “low-risk regenerative investment”, “capital unlock achieved”, “certified syntropy” or “guaranteed time-positive appreciation”.

# 10. REPRODUCIBLE COMPUTATION, AI AND SECURITY

## 10.1 Computational modes

- **D deterministic:** validation rules, typed transitions, accounting identities, reproducible quant calculations, canonical source resolution, release hashes and audit replay.
- **P probabilistic:** calibrated Bayesian/stochastic uncertainty, sensitivity, dependence and scenarios with explicit parameters, versions and priors.
- **H heuristic:** LLM summaries, pattern detection, site alternatives, red-team proposals and unanswered-question lists, always labelled proposals.
- **Q quantum or quantum-inspired:** research/shadow; admissibility only if it beats an appropriate classical baseline, followed by D verification.

Every P/H/Q output that may affect a gate passes the D boundary, which checks source identity, schema, evidence status, provenance, uncertainty, policy, conflicts and required human authority.

## 10.2 Threat model

At minimum consider forged geo-tagged imagery, fabricated sensor observations, compromised reviewers, vendor/model prompt injection, stale or cherry-picked satellite scenes, duplicate environmental claims, privileged data extraction, legal-doc substitution, land-title spoofing, artificial source-authority escalation, API write-path bypass, correlated multi-site disasters, branch/release supply-chain compromise and misleading investor-facing copy.

Mitigations: separation of duties, provenance and calibration, reviewer challenge, privacy-minimised storage, cryptographic hashes (integrity not ecological truth), RBAC, signed release references where adopted, source and claims registry checks, immutable audit trail, tested restore, no unauthorized write path and explicit incident/rollback rules.

## 10.3 Offline-first and interoperability

The decision-support pipeline must remain operable with bounded offline/local evidence capture and later controlled synchronization. Holochain can anchor agent-centric provenance where it beats a simpler signed-database approach; it cannot prove accuracy, unique ecological impact, valuation or rights by itself.

TOPOS or any external partner adapter accepts only documented, permissioned imports, field-level mapping, hashes and source labels. Imported vendor interpretations never become verified indicators or legal opinions by transport.

# 12. DETERMINISTIC POLICY AND STATE TRANSITIONS

## 12.1 State distinction

Maintain separate lifecycle fields:
- **source state:** PROPOSED / ACTIVE / SUPERSEDED / HISTORICAL / RETIRED;
- **application work state:** INTAKE / RECONNAISSANCE / SHADOW / REVIEW / READY_FOR_HUMAN_DECISION / CLOSED;
- **evidence state:** inherited from Evidence Spine;
- **risk state:** UNASSESSED / NR / SHADOW_ESTIMATE / REVIEWED_FOR_DEFINED_USE;
- **claim state:** UNREGISTERED / HYPOTHESIS / OBSERVED / REVIEWED / ADMITTED_WITH_SCOPE / REJECTED;
- **capital state:** NOT_APPLICABLE / HOLD / HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED / LAWFUL_CLOSING_RECORDED / BLOCKED.

An allowed transition in one dimension cannot silently upgrade another.

## 12.2 Decision predicates (application examples)

**Territorial reconnaissance accepted** only if territory scope, lawful data access and source provenance are identified.

**Baseline-ready** only if unit, measurement plan, comparator/counterfactual and missing-data rules are frozen.

**Risk decision-reviewable** only if material risks and ultimate risk bearers are disclosed; unrateable probability stays NR and triggers a qualitative stress review, not a guessed number.

**Claims publication allowed** only when every material claim is registered, appropriate evidence maturity and review are present, and proposed wording is within scope.

**Capital-ready for human review** only when relevant project G0–G5, legal/title/water, downside liquidity, external review, risk ledgers and disclosure gates meet the parent application's requirements.

**Financing closed** is exclusively an externally supported legal/human factual record; a software result cannot set this as self-certified fact.

Default on material conflict or missing mandatory evidence: **HOLD / REJECT / UNKNOWN / NR**, preserving the reason and competent reviewer.

## 15.4 Negative findings and retirement rules

A negative result is not treated as a mere opportunity for more documentation. Simplify or retire the offending component if, at the registered review:
- no credible buyer/payer finds added value;
- independent reviewers cannot reproduce decisions;
- site operators cannot maintain the evidence workload;
- complexity/cost overwhelms benefits versus matched simpler diligence;
- quantified or qualitative risk quality does not improve;
- community benefits are systematically displaced by capital claims;
- source authority, legal claims or independent-assurance boundaries fail repeatedly.

Apply parent architecture self-falsifiability and complexity-budget rules. A hard safety/legal violation requires immediate HOLD independently of the number of triggers.
