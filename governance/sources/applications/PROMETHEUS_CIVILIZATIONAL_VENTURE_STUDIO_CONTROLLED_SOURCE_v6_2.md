# 4. Venture Admission Rule

Before any Civilization venture can enter BUILD, it must pass the following non-compensable admission tests:

## A. Real Problem

A defined erosion loop exists and a measurable failure mechanism is identifiable.

## B. Buyer / Payer

At least one plausible payer exists, distinct from the beneficiary where necessary.

## C. Rights

Data, land, IP, operational authority and participant rights are identifiable.

## D. Evidence Path

A baseline, comparator or bounded exploratory method can be defined.

## E. Economic Independence

The base case does not require speculative token appreciation, unverified environmental credits, unconfirmed grants or unverified regenerative premium.

## F. Risk Transparency

Material risks and Ultimate Risk Bearers can be represented through RAVEL.

## G. Legal Feasibility

No required activity depends on a legal classification that has not been identified.

## H. Complexity Test

The PROMETHEUS-enabled workflow must have a plausible path to outperform a simpler alternative.

Failure of a non-compensable gate means `HOLD`, `REDESIGN` or `KILL`, regardless of opportunity score.
