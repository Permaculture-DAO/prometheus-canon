## 3. RTC-OS mission and workflow

Territory identification → pre-development intelligence → local rights/stakeholders → alternative regenerative designs → baseline and counterfactual → source-bound evidence → MRV / causal admissibility → PRU where applicable → Aeterna method + Ravel risk analysis → RAP where applicable → independent valuation and review → legal wrapper → human capital decision → deployment and stewardship → repeated audit and adaptive control.

**Non-bypassable:** evidence before value, UNKNOWN != ZERO, loss reduction != loss allocation, transferred risk != eliminated risk, human/legal authority, no-double-counting, independent assurance and complexity budget.

## 4. Product surfaces and minimum deliverables

1. **Territorial Reconnaissance Brief:** site polygon, biophysical/civic constraints, candidate intervention, knowledge gaps.
2. **Territorial Determination Dossier (TDD):** baseline, counterfactual, competing designs, stakeholder/rights map, scope and explicit stop/redesign triggers. TDD is a design decision dossier, not a certification.
3. **Regenerative Intervention Plan:** OHE design, operator, maintenance, cost, water, labor, impact mechanisms.
4. **Evidence and Claim Pack:** evidence IDs, methods, hashes, provenance, uncertainty, claims wording.
5. **Aeterna/Ravel Risk Pack:** gross and residual loss, two ledgers, stress cases, Ultimate Risk Bearer Map, transfer recoverability.
6. **Capital Readiness Dossier:** independent value range, base case with speculative uplifts = 0, cash flow, rights, investor protection, lawful wrapper and human committee decision.
7. **Continuous Stewardship Dashboard:** indicators, evidence freshness, incident handling, adaptive intervention. Dashboard is non-certifying.
