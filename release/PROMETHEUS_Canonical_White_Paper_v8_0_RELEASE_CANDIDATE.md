## Canonical definitions retained

- **OHE:** minimum deployable regenerative unit; not automatically a financial asset.
- **MRV:** verification-first measurement/reporting/verification substrate; not certification or truth by itself.
- **PRU:** analytical representation of verified regenerative performance; not land, yield, token, security or right.
- **RAP:** aggregation of PRU-relevant units for portfolio analysis; not a security or liquidity by itself.
- **TRBK / access layer:** candidate consumptive utility/access architecture, legally gated and fully decoupled from PRU/RAP ecological value.
- **AI:** assistive only; never controlling authority for ecological truth, legal admission or financial admissibility.
