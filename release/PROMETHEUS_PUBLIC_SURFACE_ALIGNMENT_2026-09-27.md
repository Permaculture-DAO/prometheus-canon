**Architecture specified. Sicily Genesis is PRE-PILOT. Experimental Design v0.1 exists, but preregistration is not frozen; G-SIC-01 population is suspended; comparator topology, laboratory chemistry and power/MDE remain open. Field validation and certification are not established.**

## hApp / console UI contract

Every result view that could be mistaken for certification must expose:
- claim status;
- evidence tier;
- unresolved lowest gate;
- provenance/source reference;
- reviewer state;
- `evaluation, not certification` boundary;
- test/demo vs real-data state.

`PROMETHEUS_ALLOW_REAL=0` remains the default until the real-data integrity gate closes. Demo/test evidence must be visually and structurally distinguishable from real evidence.

2. Regeneration must be measurable and falsifiable.
3. Distributed provenance without making the runtime an authority.
4. Pilot-first science.
5. Institutional adoption through explicit gates.
